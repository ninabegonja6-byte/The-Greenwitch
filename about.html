Our story

Why The Greenwitch?

The Greenwitch is a space for young writers and artists to explore ideas, experiment with form, and share work that deserves to be read.

We believe that literature is not only something to study, but something to make: a way of noticing the world, asking questions, and finding unexpected connections.

Through poetry, fiction, essays, and visual art, The Greenwitch brings together emerging voices and makes room for curiosity, imagination, and thoughtful conversation.
