Contribute to the journal

Something to share?

We want to read what you're making.

The Greenwitch welcomes original work from student writers and artists. We're interested in pieces that are curious, thoughtful, surprising, experimental, or deeply personal.

What can I submit?

Poetry, short fiction, personal essays, literary criticism, photography, illustration, and other visual art.

Before you send your work

Please submit work that you created yourself. Include a title, the category of your work, and the name or pen name you'd like us to use.

How do I submit?

Submission instructions and the submission form will be announced here soon. Please check back for updates.
